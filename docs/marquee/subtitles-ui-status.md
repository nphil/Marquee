## What was built

- `rust-modules/screens/src/settings_subtitles.rs`: every field of the look as a row, stepped with LEFT and
  RIGHT (OK steps forward and wraps), a live preview with six samples and three scenes.
  `AssSubtitles::draw_look` and `Mapping` (player) draw the real libass frame scaled into the picture.
- `rust-modules/screens/src/settings_bazarr.rs`: typed address and key on the television's keyboard, Test
  connection, Forget; `PreferenceCmd::{BazarrLink, BazarrRead, BazarrTest}` in `src/app/preferences.rs`.
- Authored ASS "use my look": `plx_ass_set_style_override` (libass ABI 5), `ass::request_styled`,
  `text_ass::style_override`.
- `ui::table_screen::TableScreen::adjusting`: a table whose LEFT edge is the screen's, for steppers.
- Strings: `locales/*/subtitles.json`, `locales/*/bazarr.json` (generated by `/tmp/subs2-work/add_locales.py`).
